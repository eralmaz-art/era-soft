# ERA SOFT v0.5.1 — Platform Shell

Release status: **development candidate**  
Application version: **0.5.1**

## Delivered capability

ERA SOFT now opens at a platform-level application portfolio instead of entering
ERA Construction directly. The shell establishes the approved hierarchy:

```text
ERA SOFT Platform → Application → Module → Feature
```

The platform page shows truthful Russian lifecycle states:

- ERA Construction — «Активно»;
- ERA Concrete — «Скоро»;
- ERA Education, HR, Documents and AI — «Запланировано»;
- ERA Finance and the executive dashboard — «Основа готова»;
- Administration — «Активно».

Selecting ERA Construction switches to its contextual navigation. Returning to
ERA SOFT restores the platform application portfolio.

## Explicitly not included

- no new DocTypes;
- no business workflows;
- no Concrete, Education, HR, Documents or AI functionality;
- no ERPNext or Frappe core changes;
- no production data migration.
