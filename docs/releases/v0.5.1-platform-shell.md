# ERA SOFT v0.5.1 — Platform Shell

Release status: **stable platform release**
Application version: **0.5.1**

## Delivered capability

ERA SOFT now opens at a platform-level application portfolio instead of entering
ERA Construction directly. The shell establishes the approved hierarchy:

```text
ERA SOFT Platform → Application → Module → Feature
```

The platform page separates three permanent product categories:

- business applications: ERA Construction, ERA Concrete and ERA Education;
- platform services: ERA Finance, ERA HR, ERA Documents and ERA AI;
- management: executive dashboard and administration.

Each entry is presented as a product card with a truthful lifecycle state and a
short explanation of its role. A product roadmap shows the completed platform
foundations, the current Construction MVP stage, and the next procurement slice.

Selecting ERA Construction switches to its contextual navigation. Returning to
ERA SOFT restores the platform application portfolio.

## Existing application capability

This repository already contains the separately released Construction MVP Part
1: Project → Need → Approval → ERPNext Purchase Request. Platform Shell does not
add or change its business rules.

## Explicitly not included in Platform Shell

- no new DocTypes;
- no business workflows;
- no Concrete, Education, HR, Documents or AI functionality;
- no ERPNext or Frappe core changes;
- no production data migration.
