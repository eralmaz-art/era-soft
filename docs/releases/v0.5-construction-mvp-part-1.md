# v0.5-construction-mvp-part-1

Status: **Released**<br>
Release date: **2026-08-08**<br>
Application version: **0.5.0**

## Employee Outcome

A Project Manager can record a material need for one construction object,
explain why and when it is required, send it for independent approval, receive a
return or rejection reason, and obtain an approved ERPNext Purchase Request
without maintaining an Excel request register or circulating a paper form.

## Delivered Capability

```text
Project
  → Purchase Need
  → independent approval
  → submitted ERPNext Purchase Request
```

- New ERA Purchase Need form linked to one company and one open Project.
- Required business reason, required-by date, priority, material quantities,
  unit, and target Warehouse.
- Workflow states for draft, pending approval, approved, returned, and rejected.
- Separate Project Manager and Construction Approver roles.
- Self-approval is prohibited for ordinary users.
- A return or rejection requires a decision reason.
- Approval creates and submits one standard ERPNext Material Request of type
  Purchase and preserves a two-way source link.
- Project, Item, UOM, and Warehouse values are reused from ERPNext.
- Construction and Procurement workspaces expose Purchase Needs.
- An open Project provides a **Create Purchase Need** action.
- Russian labels and workflow actions are included.

## Roles

| Role | Capability |
|---|---|
| ERA Project Manager | Create, edit, submit, revise, and resubmit Purchase Needs for permitted projects |
| ERA Construction Approver | Review, approve, return, or reject a pending need |
| System Manager | Configure access and support the process; not the normal business approver |

Users also require appropriate company/project visibility. The release does not
grant unrestricted access to all ERA companies or projects.

## Standard ERPNext Reuse

ERA SOFT does not replace ERPNext procurement. The capability reuses:

- `Project` as the construction object;
- `Item` and `UOM` as material identity;
- `Warehouse` as the required destination;
- submitted `Material Request` type `Purchase` as the approved Purchase Request.

The custom ERA record owns only the pre-purchase need, business reason, decision
history, and source relationship. No ERPNext or Frappe core files are modified.

## Manual Process Replaced

This release replaces the first manual control point:

- Excel or paper list of project material needs;
- chat-based approval without a stable status;
- manual re-entry of an approved need into a Purchase Request;
- separate lookup to determine why the request was returned or rejected.

## Installation and Upgrade

From the supported bench:

```bash
bench --site era.localhost migrate
bench --site era.localhost clear-cache
```

Assign the Project Manager and Construction Approver roles to different real
employees before operational use. Validate company and project access with each
employee account.

## Deliberate Limitations

- This part supports material needs only; service and subcontractor acceptance
  are later vertical slices.
- Budget availability and budget exception decisions are not implemented yet.
- Supplier selection, Purchase Order, delivery, invoice, and payment are outside
  this part.
- No mobile/offline flow is included.
- The release does not migrate historical spreadsheet or paper requests.

## Upgrade Safety

- All custom code and configuration remain inside `era_soft`.
- ERPNext `Project`, `Item`, `Warehouse`, and `Material Request` are extended only
  through supported links, permissions, hooks, and public document APIs.
- No class override, whitelisted-method override, monkey patch, or core source
  modification is used.
