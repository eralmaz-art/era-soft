# ERA SOFT Product Strategy

Status: **Draft — Product Owner review**<br>
Horizon: **5–10 years**<br>
Scope: **Product strategy, not implementation plan**

## Why ERA SOFT Exists

ERA Group should not have to choose between flexible spreadsheets that cannot
be trusted and generic enterprise software that does not reflect how its people
make decisions.

ERA SOFT exists to become the daily operating environment through which ERA
companies understand reality, coordinate work, make accountable decisions, and
learn from results. Its purpose is not to digitize every existing form. Its
purpose is to reduce uncertainty, delay, duplication, and avoidable management
effort while preserving evidence and responsibility.

## Strategic Mission

Create one modular operating platform that helps every ERA role make the next
important business decision quickly, safely, and from trusted facts.

## Who ERA SOFT Is For

ERA SOFT is built first for ERA Group and the people responsible for its daily
business outcomes:

- owners and executives deciding priority, risk, investment, and cash;
- business and construction directors coordinating portfolios and constraints;
- project and functional managers controlling delivery within authority;
- procurement, finance, HR, sales, and operational managers running specialist
  processes;
- site, plant, warehouse, education, and other frontline teams creating the
  facts on which management depends;
- controllers and auditors protecting traceability and policy;
- administrators maintaining safe access and configuration without becoming
  business decision makers.

An external commercial product may be considered later, but it is not an
assumption of the current strategy. ERA's own operating quality and proven
outcomes come first.

## Problems ERA SOFT Must Solve

Over the next five to ten years, ERA SOFT must reduce or eliminate:

1. Parallel spreadsheets that disagree about the same business fact.
2. Decisions made from stale summaries without source evidence.
3. Work blocked because needs, approvals, delivery, acceptance, or payment are
   discovered too late.
4. Different departments using incompatible language for the same event.
5. Responsibility hidden inside chats, verbal instructions, and private files.
6. Management time spent assembling reports instead of resolving exceptions.
7. Business applications that duplicate identity, permissions, files, audit,
   notifications, and interface behavior.
8. Large ERP menus that expose technical structure instead of a user's job.
9. Changes that erase history or make management numbers impossible to explain.
10. Growth that requires rebuilding the operating system for every new ERA
    company or business line.

## Ten-Year Product Outcome

ERA SOFT becomes the shared business operating platform for ERA Group. A user
enters one trusted product, sees only the companies, applications, objects, and
decisions relevant to their role, and can move from a management signal to its
source evidence and accountable owner.

Independent applications serve Construction, Concrete, Education, Finance, HR,
Procurement, CRM, Documents, AI assistance, and future businesses. They share
one platform and experience system without becoming a single business-logic
monolith.

The product identifies important change and risk early enough to influence the
outcome. It does not merely report what happened after the opportunity to act
has passed.

## Product Thesis

ERA SOFT will create durable value when it combines five qualities:

1. **Trusted business truth.** Important facts have one authority, consistent
   meaning, known freshness, and source evidence.
2. **Decision-centered work.** Screens, alerts, reports, and features exist to
   support named decisions or observable operating outcomes.
3. **Context before catalogue.** Users work inside a company, project, customer,
   plant, employee, institution, or other meaningful business context rather
   than navigating technical modules.
4. **Focused applications on one platform.** Business products remain
   independent while shared capabilities and interface patterns are developed
   once.
5. **Progressive intelligence.** Automation and AI are introduced only after
   authority, evidence, permissions, and evaluation are trustworthy.

## Strategic Principles

1. **Decision before feature.** No feature is admitted until the business
   decision or outcome it supports is explicit.
2. **One fact, one authority.** ERA SOFT never creates a second operating truth
   for convenience.
3. **Traceability is part of the product.** Users must understand where an
   important number came from, who owns it, and why a decision was made.
4. **Business language before software language.** Visible concepts follow
   approved ERA terminology; framework identifiers remain internal.
5. **Platform stable, applications focused.** Cross-cutting capabilities are
   governed centrally; application business logic stays within its owner.
6. **Applications are peers.** No application becomes a hidden prerequisite for
   an unrelated application.
7. **Small complete outcomes beat broad incomplete modules.** Delivery proceeds
   through end-to-end vertical scenarios that can be used and reconciled.
8. **UX and UI precede Data Model.** The user decision, flow, states, and
   interface intent are approved before records and relationships are designed.
9. **Configuration and reuse precede customization.** Proven ERPNext and Frappe
   capabilities are reused when they satisfy the approved business contract.
10. **Exceptions are visible; normal work stays calm.** Attention is reserved
    for risk, missing evidence, delay, and decisions requiring action.
11. **History is never silently rewritten.** Correction, revision, cancellation,
    and reversal remain explicit.
12. **Human authority remains accountable.** Automation and AI may assist but do
    not silently approve, pay, post, or change business truth.

## What ERA SOFT Will Never Become

ERA SOFT will never intentionally become:

- a reskinned catalogue of ERPNext modules;
- a platform dumping ground for application-specific business logic;
- a network of applications that directly depend on one another;
- a parallel accounting, stock, supplier, employee, or project truth where an
  approved authoritative source already exists;
- a collection of fields, buttons, reports, or dashboards without named user
  value;
- an environment where private spreadsheets are required to complete the normal
  daily control cycle;
- a system that hides missing, stale, disputed, or unreconciled information as
  zero or complete;
- a product that sacrifices evidence, permissions, or segregation for speed;
- a custom fork of Frappe or ERPNext core;
- an autonomous decision maker for protected financial, stock, employment,
  contractual, or safety actions;
- a one-release attempt to implement every possible business capability;
- a visually impressive interface whose information does not help a user act.

## Product Portfolio Strategy

### Now — prove the operating method

1. Keep the Platform architecture boundary locked.
2. Finalize and approve the ERA Construction Business Model.
3. Complete Construction UX and UI for one vertical scenario.
4. Design the Data Model only after those product gates are approved.
5. Implement and reconcile one complete Construction scenario:

```text
Need
  → approval
  → supplier order
  → delivery or acceptance
  → supplier invoice
  → payment
```

6. Validate the scenario with a real project and named operational users.

### Next — establish a repeatable application pattern

- use evidence from the Construction pilot to improve application, module,
  feature, and platform-boundary governance;
- extend Construction only through additional complete decisions and vertical
  scenarios;
- begin the next application only when its business owner and product gates are
  ready;
- reuse platform services and Design System patterns without copying
  Construction business logic.

### Later — expand the ERA operating portfolio

- add Concrete, Education, Finance, HR, Procurement, CRM, Documents, and AI
  Assistant as independent products according to business priority;
- introduce cross-application contracts only for validated operating handoffs;
- use AI for grounded explanation, preparation, detection, and forecasting
  before considering higher-risk action;
- consider external commercialization only after ERA has proven adoption,
  operating value, supportability, and product ownership internally.

## Feature Admission Standard

A feature proposal is incomplete until it answers all of the following:

| Question | Required answer |
|---|---|
| User | Who specifically needs this capability? |
| Context | In which company, application, module, and operating situation? |
| Trigger | What event creates the need, and how often does it occur? |
| Decision or outcome | What concrete decision becomes faster, safer, or possible? |
| Required facts | What minimum information and evidence are necessary? |
| Business owner | Who is accountable for meaning and result? |
| Consequence | What changes when the user acts, defers, rejects, or does nothing? |
| Existing solution | Can current process, configuration, ERPNext, or a platform service already solve it? |
| Success evidence | How will ERA know the capability improved the outcome? |
| Boundary | Why does it belong to this feature, module, application, or the platform? |
| Exceptions | What abnormal, missing-evidence, and not-authorized outcomes exist? |
| Gate | Which Business Model, UX, UI, Data Model, and release approval applies? |

The admission statement must be written in business language:

```text
For [named user], when [trigger/context], help them decide or achieve [outcome]
using [minimum facts/evidence], so that [business consequence] is improved.
```

“Add a Comment field” fails this standard. “Help the approver understand and
preserve why a purchase need was rejected so the requester can correct it and
the decision remains auditable” passes the product-value test. Whether a field
is the right solution is decided later in UX and Data Model.

## Vertical-Slice Strategy

ERA SOFT delivers small, complete operating outcomes rather than broad partial
modules. A vertical slice must:

- begin with a real business trigger and end with a reconciled outcome;
- include normal, exception, correction, and not-authorized behavior;
- identify every human decision, owner, handoff, and source evidence;
- remain within one approved application and module boundary or use an approved
  platform contract;
- use approved UX and UI before Data Model and implementation;
- be usable by named pilot users without a shadow operational ledger;
- expose how the outcome affects management control;
- include acceptance and rollback evidence before release.

The first Construction slice is intentionally narrow: Need → Approval → Supplier
Order → Delivery or Service Acceptance → Supplier Invoice → Payment. Budget and
project context are control inputs and reconciled consequences, not a reason to
expand the slice into every Construction capability.

## Strategic Measures

Targets will be set only after baselines are measured. ERA should track whether
the product improves:

- time from operating need to accountable decision;
- time from management signal to source evidence;
- number of shadow spreadsheets required for normal daily work;
- percentage of material decisions with owner, reason, evidence, and outcome;
- completeness and freshness of management information;
- frequency and age of unresolved exceptions;
- user ability to complete the approved vertical scenario without workarounds;
- reuse of platform services and Design System patterns across applications;
- rate of production defects caused by ambiguous ownership or duplicated facts;
- adoption during the normal eight-to-ten-hour working day.

## Strategy Governance

- The Product Owner owns this strategy and approves material changes.
- Application Product Owners translate the strategy into product specifications
  without weakening platform or product principles.
- The Architecture Owner protects the locked Platform boundary and supported
  foundation.
- Process Owners approve business meaning, authority, evidence, and exceptions.
- The Design System Owner protects one coherent product experience.
- A roadmap item, executive request, or technical opportunity does not bypass
  the Feature Admission Standard or mandatory product gates.
- Strategy is reviewed when business direction materially changes, after a
  production pilot, or at least annually; it is not rewritten for routine
  feature prioritization.

## Decisions Required for Approval

Before this strategy becomes **Approved**, the Product Owner must confirm:

1. ERA Group as the primary customer and whether external commercialization is
   intentionally deferred.
2. The ten-year application portfolio direction and ordering principle.
3. The non-negotiable “never” list and AI authority boundary.
4. The first Construction vertical slice and named pilot.
5. Strategic baseline measures and accountable measurement owners.
6. Platform, application, process, architecture, and Design System ownership.

This strategy does not approve Platform P0 decisions, the Construction Business
Model, UX, UI, Data Model, implementation, migration, or release.
