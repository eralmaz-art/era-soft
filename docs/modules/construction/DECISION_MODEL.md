# ERA Construction Decision Model

Status: **Draft — Business Model gate**<br>
Scope: **Business decisions, not ERP documents or software workflows**<br>
Implementation: **not authorized**

## Purpose

ERA Construction is organized around decisions that keep an object moving
within approved cost, schedule, quality, authority, and cash constraints. This
document defines the minimum business contract for those decisions before any
screen, record, workflow, or automation is designed.

The named decision maker describes accountable business authority. Exact value
limits, alternates, and independent-review requirements remain subject to the
approved authority policy.

## Decision Principles

- A decision exists to produce a business consequence, not merely to change a
  status.
- The decision maker must understand the reason, supporting facts, missing
  evidence, alternatives, and consequence before deciding.
- Approval may include explicit conditions; conditions have an owner and due
  date.
- Returning for correction is different from rejection.
- Escalation changes the decision authority or priority, not the underlying
  business facts.
- No decision silently rewrites the budget, evidence, obligation, cost, or prior
  decision history.

## DM-01 — Authorize Project Start

**Decision**<br>
May the construction object enter active planning or execution under the
proposed mandate?

**Purpose**<br>
Establish that the object has an approved purpose, accountable leadership,
scope, constraints, and authority before it creates obligations.

**Required Inputs**

- business purpose and expected outcome;
- company and object boundary;
- initial scope, major milestones, and known constraints;
- responsible Project Manager and governance roles;
- initial funding and budget readiness;
- material legal, land, permit, design, and safety prerequisites;
- known risks and unresolved conditions.

**Decision Maker**<br>
Owner or delegated investment/project authority, with recommendations from the
Construction Director and Finance Manager.

**Possible Outcomes**

- approve start;
- approve with conditions and due dates;
- return for missing prerequisites;
- defer pending an external or funding condition;
- reject or cancel the proposal.

**Business Consequences**

- establishes accountable project leadership and the permitted lifecycle state;
- authorizes only the stated planning or execution boundary;
- does not by itself approve a budget, purchase, contract, or payment;
- creates owned conditions and escalation dates where approval is conditional.

## DM-02 — Approve Initial Budget

**Decision**<br>
Is the proposed initial cost baseline an acceptable authorization for the
defined project scope?

**Purpose**<br>
Create the stable baseline against which scope, commitment, actual cost,
forecast, and change are governed.

**Required Inputs**

- approved project scope and cost structure;
- estimate assumptions, quantities, rates, exclusions, contingency, and taxes;
- currency and effective date;
- cash and funding implications;
- known uncertainty and risk allowance;
- estimator, Project Manager, Finance, and Construction recommendations.

**Decision Maker**<br>
Owner or delegated budget authority according to project value and risk.

**Possible Outcomes**

- approve as the initial baseline;
- approve with explicit scope conditions;
- return for correction or evidence;
- defer pending scope or funding clarity;
- reject.

**Business Consequences**

- establishes one immutable approved initial baseline;
- enables budget-controlled needs and commercial decisions within authority;
- records assumptions that later revisions must explain;
- does not guarantee cash availability or approve individual purchases.

## DM-03 — Approve Budget Revision

**Decision**<br>
Should the current authorized budget change because approved scope, assumptions,
price conditions, risk, or execution expectations changed?

**Purpose**<br>
Keep the authorized cost view realistic without erasing the original baseline
or disguising unauthorized history.

**Required Inputs**

- current initial and revised baselines;
- requested changes by project, work, and cost purpose;
- reason, effective date, and initiating owner;
- effect on scope, commitment, actual cost, forecast, cash, and contingency;
- alternatives and mitigation considered;
- prior exceptions or decisions affected.

**Decision Maker**<br>
Delegated budget authority; escalated to the Owner when value, risk, or policy
thresholds require it.

**Possible Outcomes**

- approve revision;
- approve part of the revision;
- approve with conditions;
- return for analysis;
- defer;
- reject.

**Business Consequences**

- creates a new current authorized budget while preserving all prior versions;
- changes future budget-control decisions from its effective date;
- does not retroactively authorize earlier violations;
- updates forecast and funding review obligations.

## DM-04 — Approve Project Need

**Decision**<br>
Should the stated material, service, or subcontracted-work need proceed to
sourcing or an existing commercial arrangement?

**Purpose**<br>
Confirm that the need is real, timely, correctly scoped, owned, and aligned with
the object plan before procurement effort or supplier commitment.

**Required Inputs**

- object, work purpose, and cost context;
- requester and accountable owner;
- quantity or service scope and quality requirements;
- required-on-site or required-completion date;
- available material, existing orders, or alternative supply;
- budget position and schedule consequence;
- urgency, evidence, and requested exception if applicable.

**Decision Maker**<br>
Project Manager or delegated project authority, with technical confirmation
from the responsible site role and budget review where required.

**Possible Outcomes**

- approve for sourcing or order;
- approve a reduced or phased need;
- satisfy from available stock or an existing agreement;
- return for clarification;
- defer to a later work stage;
- reject;
- escalate as an emergency or budget exception.

**Business Consequences**

- authorizes procurement work, not a supplier commitment;
- establishes priority, required date, and accountable follow-up;
- exposes budget or schedule risk where the need cannot proceed;
- preserves rejected or changed need history.

## DM-05 — Select Supplier or Contractor

**Decision**<br>
Which supplier or contractor best satisfies the approved need and acceptable
commercial risk?

**Purpose**<br>
Make a defensible sourcing choice based on total business consequence rather
than price alone.

**Required Inputs**

- approved need and required dates;
- comparable scope, quantity, quality, and commercial offers;
- supplier qualification, capacity, performance, and conflicts;
- total cost and payment terms;
- delivery, warranty, retention, and contract conditions;
- budget and cash consequences;
- sole-source or emergency reason when competition is absent.

**Decision Maker**<br>
Procurement Manager within delegated authority, with project, technical,
finance, contract, or Owner approval as thresholds require.

**Possible Outcomes**

- select preferred supplier or contractor;
- select with negotiation or conditions;
- split the award with stated responsibility;
- return for clarification or rebid;
- approve sole source by exception;
- reject all offers.

**Business Consequences**

- establishes the recommended commercial counterparty and rationale;
- identifies risks and conditions that must carry into commitment;
- does not create a commercial obligation until purchase approval;
- makes deviations from normal competition visible.

## DM-06 — Approve Purchase or Commercial Commitment

**Decision**<br>
May ERA commit to the proposed material purchase, service, subcontracted work,
or contract change?

**Purpose**<br>
Protect budget, authority, delivery, quality, legal, and cash interests before a
binding promise is made to a supplier or contractor.

**Required Inputs**

- approved need and supplier decision;
- final scope, quantity, quality, price basis, and currency;
- delivery or completion dates and acceptance conditions;
- payment, advance, retention, warranty, penalty, and cancellation terms;
- contract requirement and legal or commercial review;
- budget, commitment, forecast, and cash consequences;
- exceptions and conflicts requiring explicit acceptance.

**Decision Maker**<br>
Delegated purchase or contract authority according to value, risk, and terms;
the Owner decides above the delegated boundary.

**Possible Outcomes**

- approve commitment;
- approve with conditions or limits;
- approve a partial or phased commitment;
- return for negotiation or correction;
- defer;
- reject;
- escalate for budget or policy exception.

**Business Consequences**

- authorizes a defined commercial obligation and accountable supplier promise;
- changes the project's committed-cost and cash-risk position according to
  approved business policy;
- creates follow-up responsibility for delivery, acceptance, and terms;
- does not prove delivery, acceptance, invoice validity, or payment approval.

## DM-07 — Accept Material Delivery

**Decision**<br>
What part of the delivered material has ERA received into custody and accepted
against the agreed quantity, quality, and condition?

**Purpose**<br>
Protect the object from paying for missing or unacceptable material and create
a trustworthy custody and discrepancy record.

**Required Inputs**

- expected supplier, material, quantity, specification, and destination;
- actual delivered quantities and date;
- inspection, measurement, quality, and condition evidence;
- shortage, excess, damage, substitution, or missing-document details;
- authorized receiver and technical confirmation where required;
- required return, replacement, quarantine, or claim action.

**Decision Maker**<br>
Authorized Site Storekeeper or receiver, with Site Engineer or quality authority
for technical acceptance and Project Manager escalation for material impact.

**Possible Outcomes**

- accept in full;
- accept partially;
- accept with documented condition;
- quarantine pending inspection;
- reject and return;
- record discrepancy and require replacement or claim.

**Business Consequences**

- establishes accepted custody and supplier delivery performance;
- creates owned discrepancy, return, or claim actions;
- supports later obligation validation;
- does not prove consumption in construction work or authorize payment.

## DM-08 — Accept Service or Subcontracted Work

**Decision**<br>
What service or work has been completed to the agreed scope, quantity, quality,
period, and acceptance conditions?

**Purpose**<br>
Confirm technical and commercial completion before project cost and supplier
obligation are treated as supported.

**Required Inputs**

- agreed scope, measurement basis, milestones, rates, and quality conditions;
- actual measured work and reporting period;
- site, technical, test, photo, or other required evidence;
- previous acceptance, advance, retention, and change context;
- defects, incomplete scope, disputes, and correction dates;
- effect on schedule and remaining work.

**Decision Maker**<br>
Authorized Project Manager or technical acceptance authority; independent
specialist or Construction Director confirmation where risk requires it.

**Possible Outcomes**

- accept in full;
- accept a measured portion;
- accept with defects and stated remedy;
- return for evidence or correction;
- defer pending test or inspection;
- reject or dispute.

**Business Consequences**

- establishes accepted work and its management-cost consequence under approved
  policy;
- supports the valid portion of the supplier obligation;
- creates defect, dispute, retention, or corrective-work obligations;
- does not by itself authorize payment.

## DM-09 — Validate Supplier Obligation

**Decision**<br>
What amount is legitimately owed, disputed, retained, offset, or not yet due
against the supplier's claim?

**Purpose**<br>
Prevent unsupported or duplicated liabilities while preserving valid supplier
obligations and their project allocation.

**Required Inputs**

- supplier claim and commercial terms;
- accepted material, service, or work evidence;
- agreed quantities, prices, taxes, currency, and due terms;
- prior invoices, payments, advances, credits, retentions, and claims;
- project and cost allocation;
- discrepancies and dispute ownership.

**Decision Maker**<br>
Finance Manager or Accountant within delegated validation authority, with
project, procurement, contract, and technical confirmation where relevant.

**Possible Outcomes**

- validate in full;
- validate an undisputed portion;
- hold or retain a defined amount;
- return for missing evidence or correction;
- dispute or reject;
- escalate a commercial exception.

**Business Consequences**

- establishes the supported payable, due terms, and project allocation;
- preserves disputed, retained, or unsupported portions separately;
- enables payment consideration but does not set payment priority or authorize
  cash release;
- triggers reconciliation and supplier follow-up responsibilities.

## DM-10 — Approve Advance Payment

**Decision**<br>
May cash be released before corresponding material or work has been accepted?

**Purpose**<br>
Permit justified commercial advances while making the exposure, recovery path,
and responsible owner explicit.

**Required Inputs**

- approved commercial commitment and advance terms;
- business reason and alternatives;
- supplier risk and security or guarantee where required;
- amount, currency, due date, and source account;
- expected supply or work milestones;
- budget and cash consequence;
- allocation, evidence, refund, and escalation conditions;
- existing unresolved exposure to the supplier.

**Decision Maker**<br>
Delegated advance-payment authority, including Finance review and Owner approval
where value or risk thresholds require it.

**Possible Outcomes**

- approve advance;
- approve a reduced or staged advance;
- approve with security or evidence conditions;
- defer;
- return for commercial renegotiation;
- reject.

**Business Consequences**

- authorizes cash release without recognizing accepted supply or work;
- creates an unresolved supplier advance with a responsible owner and due path;
- affects cash availability and supplier exposure;
- requires later allocation, refund, recovery, or authorized resolution.

## DM-11 — Approve Payment

**Decision**<br>
Should ERA release the proposed amount to the supplier or contractor at the
proposed time and from the proposed account?

**Purpose**<br>
Balance valid obligation, contractual due date, work continuity, cash priority,
authority, and control before cash leaves ERA.

**Required Inputs**

- validated obligation or approved advance basis;
- supporting commercial and acceptance evidence;
- amount, currency, beneficiary, account, and proposed date;
- due date, discount, penalty, retention, dispute, and prior payment context;
- object, cost purpose, and allocation;
- available cash, near-term cash requirements, and payment priority;
- approval authority and segregation confirmation.

**Decision Maker**<br>
Delegated payment authority according to value, account, company, and risk;
Owner approval where thresholds require it.

**Possible Outcomes**

- approve as proposed;
- approve a partial amount or different date;
- approve with execution conditions;
- defer and reprioritize;
- return for evidence or correction;
- reject;
- escalate for cash or policy decision.

**Business Consequences**

- authorizes a specific cash release within stated conditions;
- changes cash availability, paid position, and supplier balance when executed;
- may unblock or endanger planned work depending on timing;
- does not change accepted cost merely because payment occurred.

## DM-12 — Approve Business Exception

**Decision**<br>
May a named business action proceed outside an approved budget, policy, evidence,
competition, sequence, or authority rule?

**Purpose**<br>
Handle genuine urgency or abnormal conditions without hiding risk or turning the
exception into an uncontrolled normal path.

**Required Inputs**

- rule and normal path being departed from;
- reason, urgency, and alternatives considered;
- object, value, scope, and duration;
- evidence available and missing;
- budget, cash, schedule, quality, legal, and control consequences;
- risk owner, resolution action, and due date;
- history of similar exceptions.

**Decision Maker**<br>
Authority explicitly designated for the affected rule and risk, independent of
the requester where required.

**Possible Outcomes**

- approve once with conditions;
- approve a reduced scope or duration;
- require compensating control;
- defer or escalate;
- return to the normal path;
- reject.

**Business Consequences**

- permits only the stated departure for the stated period and scope;
- preserves the original rule, budget, and decision history;
- creates owned corrective and review obligations;
- repeated exceptions trigger process-owner review.

## DM-13 — Close Project

**Decision**<br>
May the construction object leave active control and enter formal closeout or
closed status?

**Purpose**<br>
Confirm that delivery, commercial, material, financial, evidence, and residual
risk responsibilities are complete or explicitly accepted.

**Required Inputs**

- approved scope and completion or handover evidence;
- final physical progress and outstanding defects;
- budget, commitment, actual cost, forecast, and variance position;
- open purchases, claims, contracts, payables, advances, and retentions;
- site material balances, transfers, returns, and unresolved discrepancies;
- required legal, quality, safety, warranty, and project documents;
- residual risks, owners, values, and resolution dates;
- lessons and final management review.

**Decision Maker**<br>
Owner or delegated project-close authority, with formal recommendations from
the Project Manager, Construction Director, Finance, Procurement, and Cost
Control.

**Possible Outcomes**

- close the object;
- close with accepted residual obligations and named owners;
- enter a controlled closeout period;
- return for reconciliation or evidence;
- defer because material risk remains;
- reopen a previously closed object through explicit authority.

**Business Consequences**

- ends normal new commitments and limits actions to approved closeout activity;
- fixes the final management view subject only to explicit later corrections;
- transfers residual obligations to named owners and review dates;
- preserves project history for warranty, audit, learning, and portfolio
  comparison.

## Decisions Required Before Approval

The decision model is structurally complete but its authority model is not yet
approved. Before **Business Model Approved**, ERA must confirm:

1. Named Product Owner and process owners.
2. Decision authorities, value and risk thresholds, alternates, and escalation.
3. Required independent review and permissible role combinations.
4. Management-cost, commitment, budget, progress, acceptance, advance,
   retention, exception, and closeout meanings.
5. A real pilot in which the inputs, outcomes, and consequences of each
   applicable decision are validated.

This document does not authorize Data Model, DocTypes, permissions, workflows,
automation, accounting behavior, screens, or implementation.
